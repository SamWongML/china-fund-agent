How fields were chosen

  • Anchor: China's 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-30) names AI Plus (multimodal AI, agents, embodied AI); future industries (quantum, biomanufacturing, brain-computer interfaces, 6G, nuclear fusion); strategic emerging sectors (integrated circuits, aerospace, biomedicine, robotics, new materials, next-generation IT). Business Standard, MERICS
  • Edge: policy-supported Chinese firms earned a risk premium that vanished when support stopped (Sinclair & Zhang via VoxChina). Thematic ETFs launched after hot runs did worst (Ben-David et al. via Evidence-Based Investor).
  • Filter: active only where the 2026-10-15 ETF snapshot has at least 3 liquid index families; otherwise watch-only.

Active

  • ai_compute: AI Plus is national policy; many liquid families (AI, ChiNext AI, telecom, compute, software). Watch the launch count: ChiNext and STAR AI families listed many new products in the last year.
  • robotics: robotics and embodied AI are both named; robot ETFs from many issuers track mostly one index, so machine-tool families fill the other slots.
  • chips_equipment: full-chain IC self-sufficiency is a top priority; equipment and STAR chip families are the most liquid thematic products in the snapshot. Likely clusters with ai_compute.
  • space_satellites: aerospace and 6G priorities; satellite internet scaling. Stage emerging.
  • innovative_drugs: biomedicine and biomanufacturing priorities; A-share products only.
  • grid_power: grid investment and data-centre power demand; the steadier way into the AI build-out. Power ETFs show a recent launch wave.
  • new_materials: new materials priority plus rare-earth export controls. Stage emerging.

Watch-only (no liquid onshore products yet)

  • quantum, brain_computer: named future industries, news tracked until products list.
  • low_altitude: pilot programmes; only very illiquid 通用航空 ETFs.
