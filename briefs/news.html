Top 5 market-moving

  1. The CPC General Office and State Council General Office issued a guideline on merging tech and industrial innovation (《关于推动科技创新和产业创新深度融合的意见》). It targets breakthroughs in integrated circuits, machine tools, high-end instruments, advanced materials and bio-manufacturing. It also backs frontier fields: quantum, hydrogen and fusion, brain-computer interfaces, embodied AI and 6G. Small tech firms get a higher R&D tax deduction. This is positive for semis, robotics and new materials themes.
  2. Government bonds rallied on expectations of a new policy-rate anchor. The 10-year yield fell to 1.62% and rate-bond ETFs strengthened noticeably. The PBOC's first overnight reverse repo was 80 billion yuan, with no 7-day operation. 中信建投 keeps a slowly bullish long-term view on bonds. Experts stress the anchor switch is not a rate cut.
  3. The CSRC will steadily launch a BSE 50 ETF (北证50ETF) and IPO allocation by market value on the Beijing exchange. More than 3,000 public funds already invest there. JPMorgan's parent will put 75 million dollars, about 500 million yuan, into its China fund unit. This is relevant to brokers.
  4. AI spending stays huge. Anthropic's IPO draft shows more than 600 billion dollars planned for computing. Nvidia rose 2.2% during GTC Berlin. Xiaomi plans to invest more than 200 billion yuan in hard tech over five years. Storage chip stocks rebounded onshore, and the KOSPI opened up 2.05%. This is supportive for AI and chip ETFs.
  5. The national data group (国家数据集团) was formally set up, with the three telecom carriers and two grid companies as shareholders. China Mobile's Q3 revenue was 242.3 billion yuan, down 3.4% and below expectations (Citi).
