# News digest 2026-10-20

## Top 5 market-moving
1. **The October LPR was unchanged** at 3% for the 1-year rate and 3.5% for the 5-year, the 17th month in a row without a change. 中信证券 read Monday's PBOC reforms as clear easing signals that should help market stability. The planned switch to an overnight policy rate was described as "not equal to a rate cut" (不等同降息). This is neutral to slightly positive for bonds and banks.
2. **The PBOC will run overnight reverse repos for nine working days**, from Oct 21 to Nov 2, at a fixed rate and capped at 1 trillion yuan a day. This is the first concrete step toward an overnight policy rate and a liquidity positive for bond ETFs.
3. **US chips rebounded.** The Nasdaq rose 1.11% and the S&P 500 0.41%, led by storage and semiconductors. Reuters reported that ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent ordered more than 3 million Nvidia RTX PRO 5500 cards, worth over 35 billion dollars. Korea's KOSPI still fell as much as 3% in the day, with SK Hynix down nearly 5%. This is mixed for AI and chip ETFs.
4. **The first 18 active ETFs were approved.** Analysts at 广发证券 and 瑞银证券 read 吴清's speech as positive for A-shares. Margin debt fell 6.26 billion yuan to 2.445496 trillion yuan. This is relevant to brokers and the broad market.
5. **The State Council approved setting up 国家数据集团 (a national data group)** under SASAC. Former PBOC Governor 周小川 warned that policy tools should be held in reserve against financial turmoil from failed AI investments.

## China policy and data
- **Growth:** GDP for the first three quarters rose 4.6% (National Bureau of Statistics), and Chongqing's rose 4.3%.
- **NDRC:** it backs future-industry funding with risk-sharing, but warns regions against crowding into the same industries (避免一哄而上).
- **Hydrogen:** MIIT, MOF and NDRC issued rules for hydrogen pilot programmes.
- **FX flows:** banks bought 1,989.7 billion yuan of FX and sold 1,609.8 billion yuan in September. Goods trade continued to bring large net inflows.
- **Foreign bond holdings:** foreign institutions held 3.19 trillion yuan of interbank bonds at the end of September.
- **Lottery sales:** up 27.3% in September, to 67.389 billion yuan, driven by the World Cup.
- **Treasury issuance:** MOF plans 55 billion yuan of discount treasury bills.
- **Hong Kong links:** 平安证券 says adding REITs to Stock Connect fills a key gap.
- **Robotics:** 宇树科技 warned that humanoid robots are at an early commercial stage after its shares jumped more than 30% in three days.

## United States
- Reuters poll: all 100 economists expect a Fed hike in December 2026.
- September housing data worsened, and long-end Treasury yields fell, with 7- to 10-year yields down more than 5bp.
- 6-week bills cleared at 3.975%.
- Bessent said the bond market is "performing well". The Energy Secretary is pushing to restart closed refineries.
- The dollar index rose 0.14% to 101.980. COMEX gold fell 0.5% to 4160.3 dollars an ounce.
- Netanyahu said he ordered regime change in Iran, and Iran-US mediation looks stuck.

## Rest of world
- **Japan:** the BOJ is expected to hold in October, raise its inflation outlook and hint at a December hike. Japanese investors' net JGB purchases hit a 17-year high.
- **France:** a "triple selloff" in stocks, bonds and the currency; the 10-year bond spread rose to 150bp.
- **Germany:** September PPI rose 6.7% year on year, against 6.2% expected.
- **UK:** the 2-year gilt yield rose to 4.9712%. The RAF will help defend Saudi Arabia.
- **Canada:** banks expect a 25bp hike from the Bank of Canada next week.
