# News digest 2026-10-22

## Top 5 market-moving
1. **Big-bank shares split.** 工商银行 rose more than 2% to a record high, while the regulator stressed that banks must adapt to the reform of loan-rate pricing benchmarks. This is relevant to bank ETFs (512800).
2. **The first active ETFs launch this week.** 易方达 will buy 200 million yuan of its own first active ETF, and the CSRC will roll out market-value IPO allocation on the Beijing exchange. 国金证券 says cheap broker stocks offer good value. This is positive for brokers (512880).
3. **US 20-year bond auction was weak.** 13 billion dollars cleared at a record 5.469%. A tech rout and Middle East risk hit stocks, and the US ordered non-emergency staff to leave Saudi Arabia. New Iran sanctions targeted its shadow fleet. This is risk-off for global equities.
4. **Shanghai's Q1–Q3 GDP grew 5.5%.** Output of its three leading industries rose 17.1%, with integrated-circuit manufacturing up 23.4%. High-tech exports rose 33.0%. This is supportive for the chips theme.
5. **Earnings:** Meta's Q3 revenue was 64.6 billion dollars, up 26%, while Tesla's fell 0.2%. 宁德时代 bought back nearly 800 million yuan of shares in one day. More than 130 Shenzhen-listed firms pre-announced higher profits, led by advanced manufacturing and robotics.
