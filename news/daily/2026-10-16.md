# News digest 2026-10-16

## Top 5 market-moving
1. **The State Council executive meeting (国务院常务会议), chaired by Li Qiang, called AI a key variable for future development.** It also approved a draft amendment to the Price Law (价格法) to curb "low-price disorderly competition" (低价无序竞争) and price dumping, and to regulate platform price competition. This is policy support for AI themes and for the "anti-involution" push on margins.
2. **CSRC moves.** The CSRC opened consultation on new rules for running public funds (《公开募集证券投资基金运作管理办法（征求意见稿）》), held an expert meeting on capital markets, and fined three individuals more than 500 million yuan in total for manipulation and insider trading. The SSE said it is pushing active ETFs; listed funds are worth 3.5 trillion yuan.
3. **Bond market: liquidity stayed very loose.** DR001 fell to 1.262%, its lowest since May 19. 30-year treasury futures hit a year high early on Oct 15, then fell back below 118 at the close. The PBOC also proposed new rules for interbank bond issuance.
4. **US tech fell again.** The Nasdaq was down 1.2% late in the session, and only Apple among the top 10 US companies rose. The night before, the three US indices closed higher (Dow up 1.08%). Fed Chair Warsh gave no rate signal; markets expect a hold in October.
5. **Precious metals are in a deep slide.** Gold is about 30% below its high for the year, and silver futures are nearly 60% below theirs. Experts urge caution.

## China policy and data

- MIIT, MOF and NDRC approved hydrogen pilots in 5 city clusters, including Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei.
- MOF will issue 15.5 billion yuan of treasury bonds in Hong Kong in October.
- PBOC: Deputy Governor Tao Ling backs more yuan lending abroad and trade finance. Pan met the IMF chief and EU officials.
- 金融监管总局 said it will close regulatory arbitrage in asset management and keep welcoming foreign long-term capital.
- Shanghai and Shenzhen turnover passed 1 trillion yuan by early afternoon, slightly less than the day before. Low-altitude economy stocks surged, with more than 20 hitting their limit.
- Q3 results: 嘉立创 expects profit up 79%–118%; 四会富仕 Q3 profit up 217%; 胜宏科技 plans a 150–300 million yuan buyback.
- 中信证券 recommends storage names (HDD, DRAM, NAND).
