# News digest 2026-10-19

## Top 5 market-moving
1. **The PBOC announced a package of measures at the Financial Street Forum (金融街论坛).** Governor Pan Gongsheng said the PBOC will:
   - set up, with the financial regulator and the CSRC, a liquidity support tool for large redemptions at non-bank institutions;
   - widen access to the standing lending facility;
   - study switching its main open-market operation to overnight;
   - phase out the benchmark deposit and lending rates;
   - push for links between the interbank and exchange bond markets.

   This is liquidity and market-structure support relevant to banks, brokers and bond ETFs.
2. **China's Q3 growth came in at 4.5%, beating estimates** (CNBC). Exports surged and offset weak consumer demand. 长江证券 put growth for the first three quarters at 4.6% and said exports contributed the most. Other figures:
   - Retail sales rose 1.1% in January–September and 1.1% in September.
   - Property investment fell 20.6%.
   - Industrial robot output rose 29.1%.
   - Domestic demand contributed more than 70% of growth.
3. **CSRC Chairman 吴清 set a stability-first tone** (稳字当头). He said listed companies' first-half net profit rose 19.5%, and dividends plus buybacks reached 2.1 trillion yuan this year. He promised:
   - four new measures, including a fund industry monitoring centre and stricter penalties for illegal share sales;
   - support for broker mergers;
   - adding RMB share counters and REITs to Stock Connect.

   The first active ETFs were approved on both the SSE and the SZSE (9 in Shenzhen).
4. **Asian chip stocks fell again.** The KOSPI opened down 3.17%, with SK Hynix down more than 4%. The Philadelphia semiconductor index has fallen 8 days in a row, and CXMT shares dropped more than 6% despite a profit turnaround. On the other side, 海光信息's net profit for the first three quarters rose 45.12%.
5. **The PBOC and 10 other departments issued a guideline on financing three innovation hubs:** Beijing (京津冀), Shanghai (长三角) and the Greater Bay Area. It promotes tech finance, the social-security tech fund, and cross-border settlement for software and computing-power firms. It also warns against blind, duplicate investment.

## China policy and data
- **Home prices:** 17 of 70 cities saw new-home prices rise month on month in September, 2 more than in August. Shanghai led second-hand homes, up 0.5%.
- **Manufacturing:** value added grew 5.6% over the first three quarters and makes up 25.7% of GDP. More than a third of large manufacturers now apply AI in key processes.
- **SAFE:** 朱鹤新 said SAFE plans more measures to support firms abroad, a one-stop package to ease capital-account management, and a wider pilot for securities firms' FX business.
- **Financial regulator:** 丁向群 said all financial activity will be brought under supervision and called for faster transformation of banks and insurers.
- **CSRC enforcement:** new penalty rules for illegal securities transfers take effect on Dec 1.
- **NDRC:** a draft of basic rules for the electricity spot market is open for comment.
- **State Council Information Office:** a briefing on "technology for commerce" (科技兴商) covered tech-driven expansion of services consumption.
