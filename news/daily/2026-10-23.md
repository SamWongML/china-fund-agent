# News digest 2026-10-23

## Top 5 market-moving
1. **The Nasdaq fell 2.6% on Oct 22** as popular tech stocks slid; the Nasdaq 100 was down as much as 3% during the session. Onshore AI and chip ETFs fell hard the same day.
2. **Big banks dropped.** 中国银行 fell more than 4% in the afternoon, and 农业银行, 建设银行 and 工商银行 followed. This reverses the prior day's record highs and weighs on bank and dividend ETFs.
3. **US yields fell sharply.** The 5-year yield dropped 15bp to 4.8%, and traders no longer fully price a Fed hike by year-end. The dollar index fell 0.17% to 101.608. France's 10-year yield passed 5% and moved 50bp above Italy's.
4. **Precious metals slid.** Spot silver fell 6% to 54.47 dollars.
5. **Funding and finance:** OpenAI is closing a round of up to 30 billion dollars led by UAE investors, at a 1.25 trillion dollar valuation. MOF sold 15.5 billion yuan of treasury bonds in Hong Kong. 中信证券 remains positive on domestic chip parts and equipment.
