## Top 5 market-moving
1. **China September inflation (统计局):** CPI rose 0.9% y/y, while PPI rose 5.0% y/y and 1.2% m/m. Upstream prices are running far ahead of consumer prices, which squeezes margins for downstream and consumer sectors and favours upstream and energy names.
2. **US September CPI rose 3.7% y/y (CNBC), above the 3.6% consensus.** Sticky US inflation lowers the odds of Fed easing and keeps pressure on global risk appetite and the CNY.
3. **An AI selloff hit global stocks and pushed money into Treasuries (Bloomberg).** This feeds directly into onshore AI, compute and semiconductor ETFs, which are already weak on the watchlist.
4. **The PBOC did a 700 billion yuan outright reverse repo, a 400 billion yuan net injection for October (BigGo Finance).** It reads as an easing stance: supportive for government bonds and liquidity-sensitive sectors.
5. **China's trade surplus is nearing $1tn as exports surge (several outlets), and EU leaders are calling for new trade tools against China (SCMP).** A strong export engine brings rising trade-friction risk, with the European Council expected to discuss its China stance.
