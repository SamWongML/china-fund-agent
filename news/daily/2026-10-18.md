# News digest 2026-10-18

_Sunday: a quiet news day, mostly previews of the week ahead and geopolitics._

## Top 5 market-moving
1. **The Financial Street Forum opens Monday.** PBOC Governor Pan Gongsheng, 丁向群 of the financial regulator (金融监管总局), CSRC Chairman 吴清 and 朱鹤新 give keynotes, and major policy is expected. This is the week's main policy event for brokers, banks and the broad market.
2. **September data and Q3 GDP land Monday at 10:00**, with a press conference on the economy. China's rail passenger trips hit a record 3.71 billion in the first three quarters, a small positive for consumption.
3. **Rare-earth consolidation:** 盛和资源 plans to transfer 51% of four rare-earth smelting and separation subsidiaries to 中稀发展 (a China Rare Earth Group unit). The state is tightening its control of supply, which is relevant to the new_materials theme.
4. **Korea's finance minister expects the chip boom to last at least into 2027.** That is a counterweight to the US AI hardware selloff. 中信证券 says long-end pricing has shifted from inflation to the growth and AI cycle.
5. **Middle East tension continues:** more Saudi-Houthi strikes, Iran preparing responses, Pakistan briefly closing a border trade crossing with Iran. Oil risk stays elevated.
