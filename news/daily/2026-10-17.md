# News digest 2026-10-17

## Top 5 market-moving
1. **US tech sold off hard on Friday.** The Nasdaq fell 2.14%, the S&P 500 1.09% and the Dow 0.34%, with storage and chip stocks leading the drop. Among US tech stocks worth more than 1 trillion dollars, only Apple rose this week; optical communications and storage were hit hardest. This is more pressure on onshore AI, compute and chip ETFs on Monday.
2. **Oil spiked.** Brent rose 3.8% to 110 dollars a barrel, its first time at that level since May 20. Shipping security in the Strait of Hormuz is getting worse, with 6 ships attacked in one day. This supports energy and chemicals, but it is another inflation risk.
3. **The US deficit hit a five-year high.** The FY2026 deficit was 1.992 trillion dollars against 1.775 trillion the year before, and record interest costs reached 1.36 trillion. China cut its US Treasury holdings to 591 billion dollars in August. Fed hike bets for October eased, and the dollar is set for its worst week in six.
4. **Health insurance policy:** the National Healthcare Security Administration's 15th Five-Year Plan for medical insurance (《全民医疗保障"十五五"规划》) calls for bringing new technologies, drugs and devices into commercial health insurance coverage. This is positive for innovative drugs and medical devices.
5. **Trump waived several Russia sanctions** under the Graham sanctions act. This is a geopolitical shift worth watching for energy and commodities.
