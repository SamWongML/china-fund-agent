# News digest 2026-10-15

## Top 5 market-moving
1. **The global AI hardware selloff deepened.** The Philadelphia semiconductor index fell 4%, to 11203. Its members have lost more than 2 trillion dollars of market value over the past seven trading days. US storage stocks slid (闪迪 and 希捷 down more than 8%, 美光 down more than 5%), Korea's KOSPI closed at a 6-month low, and CoreWeave's AI bonds fell to 81 cents. This is direct pressure on onshore AI, compute and chip ETFs.
2. **US September PPI came in hot.** Economists warned the Fed may hike again, and UBS now forecasts a March hike. Treasury yields still fell after the data (10-year down 11bp to 5.08%, 2-year down 10bp to 4.69%), and the dollar eased from an 18-month high.
3. **The PBOC restated its stance at the G20.** Governor Pan Gongsheng (潘功胜) said the central bank will keep "moderately loose" (适度宽松) policy, that global imbalances can't be fixed through exchange rates, and that geopolitics is the biggest uncertainty. This supports bonds and liquidity-sensitive sectors.
4. **The IMF urged China to deploy bigger fiscal stimulus.** A Reuters poll expects Q3 growth to pick up on stimulus, keeping the 2026 goal within reach. Q3 GDP is due Monday.
5. **Gold bounced.** Spot gold rose 1.31% to 4159.07 dollars and silver 2.19%, helped by a weaker dollar and lower yields. The World Gold Council noted strong official gold buying in Q3.
