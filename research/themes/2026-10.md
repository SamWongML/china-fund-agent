# Theme scout 2026-10 (seed)

First watchlist, written with the harness change that added themes. Full research, with sources, is in the
project files: `research/theme_watchlist/Theme watchlist and probability advice.md`.

## How fields were chosen
- Anchor: China's 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-30) names AI Plus (multimodal AI, agents, embodied AI); future
  industries (quantum, biomanufacturing, brain-computer interfaces, 6G, nuclear fusion); strategic emerging
  sectors (integrated circuits, aerospace, biomedicine, robotics, new materials, next-generation IT).
  [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/technology/artificial-intelligence/beyond-ai-china-bets-on-quantum-chips-and-fusion-in-five-year-tech-plan-126092900659_1.html),
  [MERICS](https://merics.org/en/merics-briefs/merics-china-essentials-special-issue-chinas-next-five-year-plan)
- Edge: policy-supported Chinese firms earned a risk premium that vanished when support stopped
  ([Sinclair & Zhang via VoxChina](https://voxchina.org/show-3-338.html)). Thematic ETFs launched after hot runs
  did worst ([Ben-David et al. via Evidence-Based Investor](https://www.evidenceinvestor.com/post/more-evidence-that-specialised-etfs-are-best-avoided)).
- Filter: active only where the 2026-10-15 ETF snapshot has at least 3 liquid index families; otherwise watch-only.

## Active
- **ai_compute**: AI Plus is national policy; many liquid families (AI, ChiNext AI, telecom, compute, software).
  Watch the launch count: ChiNext and STAR AI families listed many new products in the last year.
- **robotics**: robotics and embodied AI are both named; robot ETFs from many issuers track mostly one index, so
  machine-tool families fill the other slots.
- **chips_equipment**: full-chain IC self-sufficiency is a top priority; equipment and STAR chip families are the
  most liquid thematic products in the snapshot. Likely clusters with ai_compute.
- **space_satellites**: aerospace and 6G priorities; satellite internet scaling. Stage emerging.
- **innovative_drugs**: biomedicine and biomanufacturing priorities; A-share products only.
- **grid_power**: grid investment and data-centre power demand; the steadier way into the AI build-out. Power
  ETFs show a recent launch wave.
- **new_materials**: new materials priority plus rare-earth export controls. Stage emerging.

## Watch-only (no liquid onshore products yet)
- **quantum**, **brain_computer**: named future industries, news tracked until products list.
- **low_altitude**: pilot programmes; only very illiquid 通用航空 ETFs.
